# PC-303: show each pet's age on the owner's page

Step 8b's ticket (e23), agreed with Prem on 2 November. It comes with its decisions made, so it skips the requirement review (steps 2 and 3): the short path starts at the author. `seed/decisions.md` is the same decisions as the change's log.

> **PC-303: Show each pet's age on the owner's page**
>
> Reception wants to see each pet's age on the owner's page, next to its birth date, so they can tell a puppy from an old dog at a glance.
>
> Decided:
> 1. One year or older: whole years ("1 year", "3 years"). Under a year: whole months ("1 month", "5 months"). Under a month: "under a month".
> 2. Age as of today, worked out when the page is shown. Nothing new is stored: no schema change.
> 3. A birthday counts on the day itself: a pet born 2023-11-02 is 3 years old on 2026-11-02 and 2 the day before. Born on 29 February: its birthday is 1 March in a year with no 29 February.
> 4. A pet with no stored birth date shows no age.
> 5. The label is "Age". The texts are English, in `messages.properties` only; the other languages fall back to it until a translation ticket.
> 6. Any further decision: stop and say so.

The draft said "column heading"; the owner's page lists each pet's details as label and value pairs, so it is the label (decision 5).

Why this ticket: the risk floor rates a change bounded when it touches only Java code, templates, messages and tests (`sensors/risk-rules.txt`). PC-301 was critical in every case, so e22 could not show the rung. The birthday rule (decision 3) is the edge a reviewer, and a planted flaw, can work on.

## Round 1 (2 November): the ticket was not settled

`runs/clean-round1/`. e14 wrote the change and listed six open questions; e17 stopped, as D6 asked, with only `PetAge` written; all four reviews blocked the unfinished change. The questions were real: D5 conflicted with `I18nPropertiesSyncTest` (every language file must have every key), and the ticket did not say what a month is when its day is missing, what a birth date after today shows, whether the label stays without an age, which time zone is today, or whether the glossary changes. Prem answered them as D7 to D12 (`seed/decisions.md`), and the glossary was extended.

The lesson for class: a bounded floor says what a change touches, not that its requirement is settled. The ticket was written without reading the code, and it skipped steps 2 and 3; the author found in one run what the requirement review exists to find.

## Round 3 (2 November): the rung said no, for a real reason

`runs/clean-round3/` (`./run.sh resume gate-round2.md clean-round2`). e21 finished every task with no stop. All four reviews found the same weakness: `todayIsTheDateInTheServersTimeZone` sets Pacific/Kiritimati (UTC+14) and never compares with UTC, so from 00:00 to 10:00 UTC a controller using UTC's date would pass too. Codex: BLOCK, with a JShell check, "No product decision is needed ... already specified (design.md:62)". Opus: DISCUSS in 3 of 3. Opus 3 of 3 also: the language test never shows the page's language changed (DISCUSS once, AUTO-FIX twice). The author had written the time-zone weakness in its self-review, which no reviewer sees.

The rung was right to refuse. The fix needs no decision, so by the stop rule it goes back to the author and a fresh review checks it (round 4).
