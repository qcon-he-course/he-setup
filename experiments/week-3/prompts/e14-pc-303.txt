This project follows two conventions for scenarios and tests:
1. Each scenario ends with a last line `- **CHECKED AT** <level>`: the lowest level at which its outcome can be observed, from unit < form < database < mysql-postgres < concurrency < manual (manual only with a recorded reason). Most scenarios are checked at unit; fewer at each level up.
2. A test says which scenario it shows by its name alone, with no comment: the method's name is the scenario's title in lowerCamelCase, punctuation and apostrophes dropped, and more words may follow. A test's level comes from its class: @WebMvcTest is form; @SpringBootTest and @DataJpaTest are database, and form as well if it drives MockMvc or HTTP; a test with no Spring test annotation is unit.
